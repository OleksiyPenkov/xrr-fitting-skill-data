## Observations outside the procedure (hypotheses, not tested)

- **Plateau step.** The measured plateau drops about 20 % between 0.22° and 0.24°. That is where the SiO2 critical angle sits (0.238° from `optical_constants`). A likely cause is uncoated glass or footprint effects reflecting at low angle. θ_max lies above the step, so the scale is anchored above the post-step plateau. The fit may then be raising Ru sigma (6.6 Å) to pull the plateau down, which would damp the high-angle fringes and produce bands 5–7. A fit with the range starting above the step and a plateau-anchored scale would test this, but it departs from sections 4 and 6, so I did not run it.
- **Shallow minima.** The metadata says no monochromator, only a parallel-beam mirror. Unfiltered Kβ or Kα2 could fill the fringe minima. The engine models a single wavelength, so this is unverified.
- **Background floor.** The engine clamps at `r_min` rather than adding a background. The smoothed tail sits near 1.08 counts against a floor of 1, which explains the constant −0.033 in band 8. It also biases the faint fringes at 2.4–3.9°.
- **Surface layer.** The procedure assumes a 15 Å hydrocarbon layer. This curve doesn't call for one. Ru sigma may absorb a thin surface layer, and the two can't be separated here.
- **Tool behavior.** The `calc_reflectivity` output has small step discontinuities at fixed angles (for example 0.208°, 0.50° and 2.36°). One of them causes the spurious first minimum in the edge block.
